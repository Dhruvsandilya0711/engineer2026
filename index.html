Prize rules closes 25 Oct 2026, 18:00 IST
  1. Two prizes, two boards. ₹2,500 for the highest single-run score and ₹2,500 for the longest streak in a single run. They are judged separately, so the same run can place on both.
  2. Your best run counts. Play as often as you like — each name appears once per board, showing only its best result. Later runs never replace a better earlier one.
  3. Ties go to whoever got there first. On an equal score or streak, the run submitted earlier ranks higher. There is no play-off.
  4. Enter under a name we can find you by. It is shown publicly on this page. 2–20 characters. We cannot pay out to a handle nobody can match to a person at the fest.
  5. One player can win both. The boards are judged independently and nothing is passed down the table. Top the score board and the streak board and you take ₹5,000.
  6. Scores are verified, not trusted. The server replays the inputs you made and derives the score itself, so a modified game changes nothing here. Submissions are limited to 20 per hour with five seconds between them.
  7. Automated play is disqualified. Scripted or computed input is not a run. Before either prize is paid, the leading entrant will be asked to reproduce a comparable result on a supervised device at the fest. Declining to is a withdrawal.
  8. We can remove an entry. Offensive names and runs we judge to be automated come off the board. The organising committee's decision on both prizes is final.
  9. Closing. The boards freeze at 18:00 IST on 25 October 2026. Winners are contacted on the email given at registration and must claim in person at the fest with a college ID.

We store a one-way hash of your IP address to rate-limit submissions. It is never shown, never shared, and cannot be reversed to your address.
